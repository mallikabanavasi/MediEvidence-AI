Textbox 2 shows each of the tissue types that are examined in the Reboot: COVID-Cancer Project data sets (the numbers in parentheses indicate which data sets the tissue type is in). As shown in Textbox 2, the data sets consist of 30 tissue types. Tissue types were chosen to distinguish the cancers instead of cancer types, as the number of tissue types was more manageable and therefore would be easier to access for the user of the application.

When a user navigates to the “COVID19-Cancer” tab of the SMART COVID Navigator, they will see a menu of tissue types, similar to how the CORD-19 risk factors are displayed, as well as another button that displays the summary information for all of the tissues in 1 pop-up, colored blue to distinguish it from the other buttons representing tissue types (Figure 6).
