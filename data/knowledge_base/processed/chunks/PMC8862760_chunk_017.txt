As shown in Textbox 1, the CORD-19 data set consists of 28 risk factors—22 of which are disease conditions, while the other 6 are patient biographical characteristics. The comprehensive clinical terminology coding systems, SNOMED Clinical Terms used by the VA and the International Classification of Diseases-tenth version (ICD-10) used by CMS, consist of thousands of more specific disease identifiers than the broader disease categories tracked by CORD-19. We mapped the identifiers in the SNOMED Clinical Terms and ICD-10 code sets to the CORD-19 risk factors to be able to match patient conditions to disease categories tracked by CORD-19 (step 2 in Figure 3). This mapping was accomplished through word matching.
