To log into the 2 API-enabled EHR systems, the application implements the OAuth2 and OpenID Connect standards to achieve secure authentication and authorization. The authentication process involves a user entering their login credentials, while the authorization process requires a request to the EHR’s server to obtain an access token. Upon receiving the access token, the application is authorized to retrieve relevant patient information. The left side of Figure 1 depicts the system architecture for the EHR access process. The login process begins when the user clicks one of the login buttons. The user is redirected to the login portal for the EHR of their choice to enter their login credentials.
