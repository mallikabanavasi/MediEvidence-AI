Standard view of the COVID-19 cancer tab of the COVID Navigator. The “Summary” button is highlighted in blue to distinguish it from the other buttons that represent tissue types.

When a tissue is clicked, a pop-up appears with 2 tabs (this figure shows the pop-up for lung tissue). The first tab (on the left) tracks patient outcomes for patients with lung cancer who contract COVID-19. The second tab (on the right) tracks drugs being tested for COVID-19 that could have an impact on cancer of the selected tissue.

When the blue “Summary” button is clicked, this pop-up appears, which displays the patient outcome summary statistics for each tissue type in a concise manner.
