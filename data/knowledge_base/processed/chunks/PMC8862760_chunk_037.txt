There are conditions for which the papers tracked by CORD-19 do not offer a definitive answer. Cancer is one of these conditions; 50% (8/16) of studies found a significant finding for severity, while 46% (6/13) found a significant finding for fatality. A possible explanation for this inconclusive result is the existence of various types of cancers in different tissues of the body, resulting in a mixed result when grouping various cancer types together. For such cases, clinicians will need to access additional information from other papers.
