ESH guidelines, however, emphasize the need  to perform, at any patient’s age, risk stratification of the hypertensive  patient on the presence of associated clinical conditions (diabetes, heart  failure, miocardial infarction, stroke, renal insufficency) and asymptomatic  target organ damage. The latter includes several measurements, based on their  undisputable predictive value for cardiovascular morbidity and mortality, large  availability and cost (electrocardiography, echocardiography, carotid  ultrasonography, pulse wave velocity estimated creatinine clearance or glomerular  filtration rate through standardized formulae, serum creatinine and  microalbuminuria) (level of evidence A). The 2023 guidelines recommend organ  damage to be searched in different organs because of the evidence that multiple  organ damage carries a worse prognosis than damage limited to a single organ.
