Accessing Multiple FHIR Servers

The SMART COVID Navigator allows the user to log into 2 EHRs: the Veterans Affairs (VA) and the Center for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). There are significant advantages to the application being able to log into multiple EHR servers.
