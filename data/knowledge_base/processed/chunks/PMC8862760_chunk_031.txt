CORD-19 Knowledge Base Analysis

First, we discuss the results from the CORD-19 knowledge base relating to risk factors. We found that out of the 816 studies examined in the risk factors section, 355 studies (43.5%) studied only how a particular disease condition affected the severity of COVID-19 progression, 328 studies (40.2%) examined only how the disease condition affected fatality, 89 studies (10.9%) examined both severity and fatality, and 44 studies (5.4%) had no entry for severity nor fatality.
