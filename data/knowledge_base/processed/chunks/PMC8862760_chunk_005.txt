For instance, electronic health records (EHRs) store all information collected in hospitals, such as blood tests, radiographs, diagnostic tests, and any biographical information about a patient, such as their age, weight, or height. This approach to medical treatment could prove to be useful in dealing with the COVID-19 caused by SARS-CoV-2, which has been ravaging the United States and the world. Currently, a vast amount of research is being conducted to understand how a patient’s underlying health conditions interact with the progression of the virus infection. For example, certain conditions such as diabetes and heart disease have been found to raise the severity and fatality rates of patients who have become infected with the virus.
